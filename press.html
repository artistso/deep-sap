DEEP SAP

Synthetic ocean sensor-fusion simulation

DEEP SAP is a tablet-first game and engineering testbed built with Rust, Bevy ECS, and WebAssembly. Players interpret noisy synthetic observations, manage track hypotheses, and reason about localization uncertainty.

Boundary: all contacts are synthetic. Public ocean observations may update environmental context only. The project is not suitable for detection, surveillance, navigation, maritime safety, targeting, or operational decision-making.

Technical elements

Deterministic truth simulation, noisy bearing observations, observation-only track association, weighted nonlinear least-squares localization, covariance-derived 95% uncertainty ellipses, estimated-state prediction, S Pen Pointer Events, offline-capable PWA shell, and validated public environmental adapters.

Approved one-paragraph description

Study noisy bearings, build track hypotheses, and evaluate uncertainty in DEEP SAP, an unclassified ocean sensor-fusion game for tablets and browsers. A strict truth/measurement boundary keeps hidden synthetic targets out of the tracking system, while optional public ocean observations affect only environmental context.
